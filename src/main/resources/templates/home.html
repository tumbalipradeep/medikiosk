Patient Case-Taking Platform

Structured health intake from kiosk to clinician

MediKiosk helps a patient describe their health concern at a kiosk, structures it into a clinical case, surfaces it to a physician for review, and keeps every step traceable. It assists clinicians — it never pretends to diagnose.

The journey

  1. 1 Identify — verify who the visitor is (local kiosk identity, no ABHA link)
  2. 2 Converse — guided conversation about the health concern
  3. 3 Scan — upload and extract report documents with provenance
  4. 4 Summarize & route — structured case with flags, sent for review
  5. 5 Consult — physician reviews, documents the assessment

For patients

A calm, language-aware way to record a health concern at a kiosk, review your answers, attach reports, and know exactly what happens next.

For physicians

A clinical workspace with the patient's own words, extracted documents, provenance, review state, and a documented assessment — not a statistics screen.

For administrators

Platform control: accounts, system settings, capability status, and a real audit trail. Admin operates the system, never the clinical truth.

Trust & safety

Built to be honest about what it can and cannot do

  • ✓ No silent AI diagnosis. AI-assisted output carries a review boundary and always reaches a clinician.
  • ✓ Conservative by default. A lab value without a printed range is reported as unknown — never guessed as normal or abnormal.
  • ✓ Provenance everywhere. Documents record the extraction method and provider; timeline events point at their source data.
  • ✓ Local by design. Demo mode uses a local identity, records consent, and transmits nothing to ABDM or an external HIS.
  • ✓ Authorization is enforced in the backend. A patient can never reach another patient's case through the frontend.
